中文版 · 方舆（Fangyu）隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026 年 11 月

方舆（Bundle ID：com.entropysniper.fangyu）是一款运行在 macOS、iOS、iPadOS 与 visionOS 上的本地地理体素世界创作工具。我们高度重视您的隐私，并遵循最小化数据原则。

1. 我们收集与处理哪些信息

  • 不追踪、不建号、不上传您的存档：方舆不要求注册账号，不集成任何第三方广告或行为追踪 SDK，不开启遥测上报。您生成的 3D 世界、地图补绘、截图与 4K 视频均保存在您的设备本地，绝不上传至我们的服务器。
  • 粗略位置（仅用于地图初始居中）：首次打开选址地图时，应用可能会向第三方 IP 地理定位接口（如 ipwho.is 或 get.geojs.io）发起一次非关联请求以获取大致城市坐标，用于定位地图初始视野。该信息不会与您的身份关联，不用于追踪，也不会被我们存储。
  • 公开地图与地理数据请求：当您搜索地点、查看底图或生成世界时，应用会直接向公开的第三方地理数据源请求所选经纬度范围的矢量、影像与高程数据，包括 OpenStreetMap（Nominatim / Overpass / 静态瓦片档）、Overture Maps、OpenFreeMap、Mapterhorn / AWS Terrain、EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless、NASA PDS / USGS 行星数据，以及（仅当您主动开启时）公开航拍激光点云（LiDAR）服务。您可在设置中关闭「自动命名地点」以减少在线地理编码请求。
  • 应用内购买（StoreKit 2）：方舆 Pro 订阅与一次性买断完全由 Apple App Store（StoreKit 2）处理与验证，我们无法访问、接收或存储您的支付卡信息或 Apple ID 账单细节。

2. 本地文件与系统权限

  • macOS App Sandbox 安全范围书签：在 macOS 上，方舆严格运行在 Apple App Sandbox 沙盒容器内。仅当您通过系统文件选择器主动授权目录（例如 Minecraft 存档目录 saves 或自定义导出位置）时，应用才会读写您选择的位置，并通过 Apple 安全范围书签（Security-Scoped Bookmark）保持授权；您随时可在应用「设置 › 导出与游戏目录」中清除该授权。
  • iOS / iPadOS 与 visionOS 文件分享：导出的世界文件保存在应用本地容器内，仅在您主动点击「在 Minecraft 中打开」或系统分享面板时移交至目标应用或「文件」中。

3. 第三方服务与数据保留

我们不运营收集用户个人数据的后端服务器。所有地图瓦片缓存、世界库和偏好设置仅存于您的设备本地，您可随时在「设置 › 存储与运行环境」中清理缓存，或卸载应用彻底删除全部本地数据。

4. 儿童隐私

方舆不向任何年龄段用户收集个人身份信息或账号数据，亦不包含社交、聊天或第三方广告内容。

5. 政策更新与联系我们

如本隐私政策有重大变更，我们将在本页面更新日期与条款内容。如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请发送邮件至：entropysniper@gmail.com。

English · Fangyu Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Fangyu (Bundle ID: com.entropysniper.fangyu) is a local-first geographic voxel world creation application for macOS, iOS, iPadOS, and visionOS. We respect your privacy and collect only the absolute minimum data required for core app functionality.

1. Information We Collect & Process

  • No tracking, no accounts, local-first creations: Fangyu requires no account registration, integrates no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and sends no telemetry. All generated 3D worlds, custom map patches, screenshots, and 4K videos are stored exclusively on your local device and are never uploaded to us.
  • Coarse location (optional initial map centering): When opening the map picker, the app may make an unlinked request to a public IP geolocation endpoint (ipwho.is or get.geojs.io) solely to center the initial map view near your approximate city. This coarse location is never linked to your identity, never used for tracking, and never stored by us.
  • Public geographic & elevation queries: When you search for a place, browse the map, or generate a world, the app requests public map, imagery, and elevation data for your selected coordinates directly from third-party public services: OpenStreetMap (Nominatim / Overpass / static tiles), Overture Maps, OpenFreeMap, Mapterhorn / AWS Terrain, EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless, NASA PDS / USGS planetary basemaps, and (only when explicitly enabled) public airborne LiDAR datasets. You may disable "Auto-Name Selections Online" in Settings to avoid reverse-geocoding queries.
  • In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2): Fangyu Pro subscriptions and Lifetime purchases are processed and verified exclusively by Apple via StoreKit 2. We never receive, view, or store your payment details or Apple Account billing records.

2. Local File Access & Sandboxing

  • macOS App Sandbox & Security-Scoped Bookmarks: On macOS, Fangyu operates strictly inside the Apple App Sandbox. It accesses directories outside its container (such as your Minecraft saves folder or an export directory) only when you explicitly grant permission via the system file picker, persisted via Apple Security-Scoped Bookmarks. You may revoke this authorization anytime under Settings › Export & Game Folders.
  • iOS, iPadOS & visionOS: Exported files remain in the app's sandbox until you explicitly share them or hand them off via "Open in Minecraft" or the system Share Sheet.

3. Data Retention & Local Control

We operate no backend database storing personal user data. Downloaded map/elevation caches, generated worlds, and preferences reside solely on your device. You can clear caches anytime under Settings › Storage & Runtime or remove all local data by deleting the app.

4. Children's Privacy

Fangyu collects no personally identifiable information from users of any age and contains no social chat, user-generated feeds, or third-party advertising.

5. Updates & Contact

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised date. If you have questions regarding privacy, please contact us at entropysniper@gmail.com.
