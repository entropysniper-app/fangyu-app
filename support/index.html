Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I restore purchases across Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro?

Fangyu Pro supports Apple Universal Purchase and Family Sharing. On any device signed into the same Apple Account (or Family Sharing group), open Fangyu Pro (or Settings › Fangyu Pro) and tap Restore Purchases to sync your unlock immediately.

2. How do I manage, switch, or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed directly by Apple. Tap Manage Subscriptions inside Fangyu or visit App Store Subscription Settings at least 24 hours before the renewal date. If you purchase Lifetime while holding an active Monthly or Yearly subscription, please cancel the renewing subscription in App Store settings to avoid duplicate billing.

3. What is included for free vs. Fangyu Pro?

Fangyu Free lets you generate Earth areas up to 1 km² with 1:1 landmark sculpting, full native Metal 3D roaming, and lossless Minecraft: Java Edition export forever. Fangyu Pro unlocks areas larger than 1 km², Moon & Mars planetary worlds, the 4K Ray-Traced Aerial Studio, Style Filters, Satellite Color Sampling, Local Map Patching, Multi-Region Stitching, and all multi-format exports (.mrpack, Creator Kit, Bedrock .mcworld, .schem, .stl, .obj).

4. How do I open exported worlds in Minecraft?

  • Java Edition (1.21.4+): On macOS, select Import to Minecraft Saves Folder and grant access to ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/saves once. Launch Minecraft → Singleplayer. If prompted about Experimental Settings (extended-height datapack), click Proceed.
  • Shader & LOD Modpack (.mrpack): Drag the .mrpack file into Modrinth App, Prism Launcher, HMCL, or PCL2; the launcher automatically fetches Sodium, Iris, Distant Horizons, and your chosen shader pack.
  • Bedrock Edition (.mcworld): On iPhone/iPad, tap Open in Minecraft. On macOS, choose AirDrop to iPad / iPhone… and open the received file in Minecraft.

5. How do I reset folder permissions or clear downloaded map caches?

Go to Settings › Export & Game Folders to change or clear the Security-Scoped Bookmark on macOS. Go to Settings › Storage & Runtime to inspect your world library size, clear cached elevation/satellite tiles, or run built-in engine diagnostics.
